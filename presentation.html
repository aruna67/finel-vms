Vehicle Management System

(Army VMS)

Enterprise Fleet Management Solution

System Overview

A secure, web-based platform engineered to modernize military logistics and fleet administration.

  • Centralized Dashboards: Live tracking of vehicles and personnel.
  • Data-Driven: MySQL/MariaDB backed architecture for reliability.
  • Multi-Tenant Architecture: Isolated databases via logical Unit ID separation.

Role-Based Access Control

Strictly implemented system boundaries corresponding with military hierarchy.

  • 👑 Super Admin (HQ)
    Full access, multi-unit broad analytics across all bases.
  • 🛡️ Unit Admin
    Full oversight capabilities solely within their specific unit.
  • 💂 Unit Officer
    Dispatch authorizations, routine operations, daily reporting.
